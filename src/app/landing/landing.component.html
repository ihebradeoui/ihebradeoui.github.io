Everything You Need to Rule the Galaxy

CosmicWorlds combines real-time multiplayer interaction with a rich 3D space simulation powered by Babylon.js and Firebase. Here's what you can do.

🌍

Interactive 3D Solar Systems

Navigate through multiple hand-crafted galaxies rendered with WebGL. Each solar system features a glowing star at the centre, planets with realistic inclined orbits, and a star-field backdrop. Drag to rotate the camera, scroll or pinch to zoom, and click any planet to inspect or claim it.

✏️

Claim & Name Your Planet

Register a free account, earn credits by watching brief ads, then rent a planet for one week. Once rented, you can give it any name and description you like. Your planet shows your identity to every visitor — cementing your place in this shared universe.

🎨

20+ Visual Customisations

Stand out from the crowd with unique visual effects. Add cosmic rings, a pulsing star aura, an orbiting moon companion, a crystalline energy shield, or a roaring flame shell. Choose up to two effects at once — more slots unlock as you build your daily streak.

🏆

Global Leaderboard

The planet leaderboard ranks every claimed planet by how long it has been held. The longer you keep renewing your planet, the higher you climb. Daily visitors compete to hold legendary planet names and prove their cosmic dominance.

🔥

Daily Login Streaks

Return to CosmicWorlds every day to build your streak counter. A longer streak unlocks additional customisation slots, letting you layer more visual effects on your planet. Missing a day resets your streak, so staying engaged is always rewarded.

🎵

Ambient Space Soundtrack

An immersive procedural music engine generates a real-time space melody using the Web Audio API. The soundtrack adapts as you navigate the galaxy, creating a unique atmospheric experience that pulls you deeper into the cosmos.

Planet Types & Shapes

CosmicWorlds features planets in seven distinct shapes and a wide variety of colours, from icy blue worlds to volcanic red giants. Explore them all in the 3D Explorer.

🌐

Sphere Planets

The classic spherical world. Smooth, perfectly rounded planets that range from tiny rocky moons to gas giants many times the size of Earth. Most habitable worlds in the galaxy take this familiar form.

🪐

Ringed Worlds

Add the Cosmic Rings customisation to any sphere planet and watch vibrant multicoloured bands shimmer around its equator — inspired by Saturn's iconic ring system, but cranked up to full galactic glory.

🎲

Cube Planets

Defying natural physics, cube planets are mysterious crystalline worlds with flat facets and sharp edges. Ancient lore suggests they were forged by an extinct civilisation that worshipped geometric perfection.

🍩

Torus Worlds

A donut-shaped planet with a hollow centre. Torus worlds have bizarre day/night cycles and extreme weather at their inner curves. Settlements cling to the outer equatorial band where gravity is most stable.

⬡

Octahedron Planets

Eight triangular faces make up this diamond-shaped world. Octahedron planets refract starlight in mesmerising ways, casting prismatic patterns across the orbital plane and dazzling all who fly nearby.

🔷

Icosahedron Planets

Twenty triangular faces give this world an almost spherical appearance, yet up close its angular facets are unmistakable. Icosahedron planets are home to the galaxy's most advanced mathematical cultures.

🧱

Cylinder Worlds

Long, cylindrical planets spin rapidly to simulate gravity on their inner surface — a favourite of deep-space colonists who need a predictable living environment far from any star's warmth.

Cosmic Facts & Inspiration

CosmicWorlds draws inspiration from real astronomy and space science. Here are some of the fascinating facts that shaped the design of the game.

☀️

The Sun Contains 99.86% of the Solar System's Mass

Every CosmicWorlds solar system places a massive glowing sun at its centre. In reality, our own Sun is so massive that all the planets, moons, asteroids, and comets combined account for only 0.14% of the total mass of our solar system. Jupiter alone makes up most of that remainder.

🌍

Orbital Inclination Creates Seasons

In CosmicWorlds, each planet orbit has a unique inclination angle, just like real planets. Earth's axis is tilted 23.5° relative to its orbital plane, which is why we experience seasons. Mars is tilted 25.2°, giving it similar seasons but lasting twice as long due to its longer year.

🪐

Saturn's Rings Are Only 10–100 Metres Thick

Despite spanning up to 282,000 km in diameter, Saturn's rings are incredibly thin — in most places only 10 to 100 metres thick. They consist mostly of ice particles, rocky debris, and dust. CosmicWorlds' Cosmic Rings customisation is inspired by this stunning natural phenomenon.

🌌

The Milky Way Contains 100–400 Billion Stars

CosmicWorlds features six explorable galaxies, each with its own unique solar system. The real Milky Way contains an estimated 100 to 400 billion stars — with the majority thought to host at least one planet. The number of potentially habitable worlds in our galaxy alone runs into the billions.

⚡

Lightning on Jupiter Strikes Three Times as Powerfully

Jupiter's atmospheric lightning bolts are estimated to be about three times more energetic than Earth's. The planet's deep storms can be larger than the entire Earth. The Plasma Sparks customisation in CosmicWorlds pays homage to these awe-inspiring electrical storms.

🔴

Mars Has the Tallest Volcano in the Solar System

Olympus Mons on Mars stands approximately 22 km high — nearly three times the height of Mount Everest. It is a shield volcano with a caldera 80 km wide. The volcanic red-orange colour palette in CosmicWorlds references Mars and other known rocky, volcanic exoplanets discovered by telescopes like Kepler and TESS.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CosmicWorlds free to play?

Yes, completely free. You can explore all the solar systems and view planet details without an account. To claim and customise your own planet you need a free account and some in-app credits, which you earn by watching short ads.

What browser do I need?

Any modern browser that supports WebGL 2.0 will work — including the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari. For the best experience we recommend Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop. The app is also playable on mobile, though a desktop screen gives you the best view of the solar systems.

How do I earn credits?

Sign in to your account, click any planet, then tap the "📺 Watch Ad for Credits" button. Each ad you watch awards 10 credits. You need 50 credits to rent a planet for one week, and 10 credits each time you save changes to a planet you already rent.

What happens when my planet rental expires?

After one week your rental expires and the planet becomes available for others to claim. You can renew your rental at any time using 50 credits before the expiry date to ensure you keep hold of your world uninterrupted.

How does the leaderboard work?

The leaderboard ranks every planet that currently has a name by how many days the name has been set. The counter starts from the moment you first rename a planet and keeps ticking as long as you keep renewing your rental. The player at #1 has held their planet name for the most days.

Can I choose which planet shape I get?

Planet shapes are assigned by the galaxy seed and cannot be changed — each planet has a fixed shape that is the same for all visitors. However you can choose any available planet of the shape you prefer by exploring the solar systems until you find one you like and it's unoccupied (or you can outbid the current occupant once rentals expire).

How do I build a streak?

Simply sign in to CosmicWorlds at least once every 24 hours. Each consecutive day you log in adds one to your streak counter. At certain streak milestones (7 days, 14 days, 30 days) you unlock an extra customisation slot, letting you apply more visual effects to your planets simultaneously.

Is my data safe?

Your account is secured with Firebase Authentication. We only store your email, hashed password, and the planet data you choose to create. We never sell your personal information. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.
