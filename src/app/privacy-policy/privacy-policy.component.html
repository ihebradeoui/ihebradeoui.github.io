Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

Welcome to CosmicWorlds ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you visit our website at ihebradeoui.github.io. By using the site you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

a. Account Information

If you choose to register or sign in, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password via Firebase Authentication (Google LLC). Your email is used solely to identify your account and is never sold to third parties.

b. Usage Data

We store planet names, descriptions, and customisation preferences that you create in the app in Firebase Realtime Database. This data is linked to your user account when you are signed in.

c. Automatically Collected Data

Like most websites, our servers may automatically collect standard log information such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and pages visited. This information is used only for site analytics and security purposes.

2. Cookies & Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

Firebase services may also set cookies to manage user sessions. For more information see Firebase Privacy & Security.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the CosmicWorlds interactive experience.
  • To save your planet customisations and credits balance across sessions.
  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdSense.
  • To maintain the leaderboard and user streak features.
  • To improve and troubleshoot the site.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with the following service providers solely to operate the site:

  • Google Firebase – authentication and database hosting.
  • Google AdSense – advertising.
  • GitHub Pages – website hosting.

Each provider is bound by their own privacy policy and data processing agreements with Google.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Account data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us (see section 8). After deletion, your data will be permanently removed from our Firebase database within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

CosmicWorlds is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.
